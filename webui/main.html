Legal Note
Sweeppea ensures US legal compliance. All sweepstakes include a free alternative method of entry (AMOE) to avoid illegal lottery status. Prizes over $5,000 with participants in FL or NY require registration. Age Gate is required for alcohol & cannabis promotions (21+). Ask Agent Zero to check compliance before launching.
